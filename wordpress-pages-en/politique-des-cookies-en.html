This page lists all the information that the RAVI store places or stores on your device when you visit it. It complements our privacy policy, which deals more broadly with how your personal data is handled.

Cookies and local storage: what are they?

The term « cookies » is commonly used to refer to all trackers that a site places on your device. The technical reality is a bit more nuanced, and we felt it was more honest to present it to you exactly as it is.

A cookie is a small file that your browser automatically sends back to the server with every request. Local storage, on the other hand, remains on your device and is only transmitted if the site explicitly requests it. RAVI relies primarily on the latter mechanism because our store is an application that communicates with its server, rather than a site that reloads page by page.

This distinction has a direct consequence for you: most of the data we store never leaves your browser, and simply clearing your browser’s storage is enough to delete everything.

Elements essential for operation

The following elements enable the store to function. Without them, it would be impossible for you to stay logged in or to keep your shopping cart contents as you move from page to page. As such, they do not require your prior consent, though you can delete them at any time.

Your login session

When you log in to your customer account, an authentication token is stored in your browser’s local storage. This saves you from having to re-enter your login details during every visit. It is deleted as soon as you log out and is never transmitted to third parties.

Local storage · kept until logout · wc_user_token

Your shopping cart ID

A technical identifier is linked to your shopping cart so the server knows which one to retrieve for you. This allows you to fill a cart without creating an account and find it intact when you return. It contains no personal data and is not linked to any identity unless you log in.

Local storage · retained until deletion · wc_cart_nonce

Your saved items

Items you mark as favorites without being logged in remain on your device only. If you subsequently create an account, this list is linked to it so it is not lost.

Local storage · retained until deletion

Your cart status

A cookie signals to the server that your cart is not empty, allowing it to adjust the display without querying the database on every page.

Cookie · session duration · woocommerce_items_in_cart

What our payment provider sets

Your order payment is processed by an external provider, which sets its own trackers. These are used exclusively to secure the transaction and detect fraud attempts; without them, card payments could not be completed. We do not have access to them and do not derive any information from them.

Device identification

A persistent identifier allows our provider to recognize the device from which a payment is attempted and thus detect fraudulent behavior. A second identifier serves the same purpose for a single payment session.

How to delete or refuse them

You retain control over all these elements via your browser settings, in the section dedicated to privacy and site data. You can delete data already stored there or block it from being stored in the first place.

However, please be aware of the implications of this choice. Deleting these items will log you out of your account and empty your current shopping cart; orders already placed remain intact and accessible via your customer area. Conversely, completely blocking storage would render the store unusable: you would be unable to add items to your cart, as the server would no longer be able to recognize it as yours.

Configuration settings vary by browser. The developers of Firefox, Chrome, Safari, and Edge each provide instructions in their respective help centers, within the sections covering cookies and site data.
