Frequently Asked Questions

Orders & Delivery

What is the delivery time?

We offer standard delivery within 5 to 7 business days. Express delivery options are also available. Delivery times are displayed at checkout. Do you ship internationally?

Yes, we ship throughout Europe. Shipping costs vary by country and are calculated automatically during checkout. Can I track my order?

Absolutely! You will receive a tracking number via email as soon as your order ships. You can track your package in real-time. How can I cancel my order?

If your order has not yet shipped, you can cancel it by contacting our support team. Once the order has shipped, please refer to our return policy.

Products & Availability

How do I know if a product is in stock?

Availability is indicated on each product page. If an item is out of stock, you can sign up to be notified when it becomes available again. Can you recommend a product?

Of course! Feel free to contact us via our contact form with any questions. Our team is here to help. Do the products come with a warranty?

All our products come with a 2-year warranty against manufacturing defects. Please check the product details for more information.

Payment & Security

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard), PayPal, and bank transfers. All payments are secured via SSL encryption. Is my payment information secure?

Yes, we use a secure payment system that complies with PCI DSS standards. Your data is never stored on our servers. Can I get an invoice?

An invoice is automatically generated after every order and sent to your email. You can also download it from your customer account.

Returns & Refunds

What is your return policy?

We offer a 30-day return period starting from the receipt of your order. The product must be unused and in good condition. How long does the refund take?

Once your return is received and verified, the refund is processed to your account within 5 to 10 business days. Who pays for return shipping?

You are responsible for return shipping costs, unless the product is defective or there was an error on our part.

Customer Account

How do I create an account?

It’s simple! Click on « Log In » at the top right, then « Create an account. » You will need to provide an email address and create a password. I forgot my password

No problem! Click on « Forgot password » on the login page and follow the instructions. A reset link will be sent to your email. How do I change my personal information?

Log in to your account and go to « My Profile. » You can update your addresses, phone number, and other personal details.
