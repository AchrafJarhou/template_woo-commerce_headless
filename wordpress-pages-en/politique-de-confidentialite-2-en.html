Information we collect

When you create an account

Creating a customer account requires us to store your username, email address, and an encrypted password. You may also choose to add your first name, last name, delivery address, billing address, and phone number. This information is visible only to you and the staff responsible for processing your orders.

When you place an order

Placing an order requires us to record details of the selected items, their prices, the delivery address, the billing address, and the shipment status. We also retain any correspondence related to the tracking of that order.

We never see or store your banking details. Payment is processed directly by our service provider, who receives your card information without it passing through our servers. We only retain a transaction ID, which allows us to match a payment to an order.

When you simply browse

You can browse the catalog and fill a shopping cart without creating an account. In this case, an anonymous session ID is associated with your cart so that it remains available to you as you navigate from page to page. This ID contains no personal data and is not linked to any identity.

When you write to us

The contact form transmits the information you enter to us so that we can reply to you. We do not use this information for any other purpose.

Why we use this information

Every piece of data we store serves a specific purpose. Your contact details and order information allow us to fulfill the sales contract between us: preparing, shipping, and tracking your package, and processing any potential returns. Retaining invoices and accounting records is a legal obligation we must comply with.

Ensuring store security, preventing fraud, and improving our service fall under our legitimate interest in operating our business effectively. Finally, sending marketing communications and placing non-essential trackers occurs only with your consent, which you may withdraw at any time.

What your browser stores

Our store relies on your browser’s local storage rather than advertising cookies. Specifically, your browser stores a session token that keeps you logged into your account, a technical ID linked to your shopping cart, and a list of items you have favorited. These items remain on your device, are transmitted only to our own servers, and are essential for the site to function: without them, your cart would empty every time you changed pages.

You can delete them at any time via your browser settings. Deleting them will log you out and empty your cart, without affecting orders already placed.

Who we share data with

We do not sell your data or transfer it to anyone else for commercial purposes. However, certain information is shared with partners without whom an order could not be fulfilled: our payment service provider, which receives the payment amount and transaction details; our carrier, which receives your delivery address; our hosting provider, which hosts the store and its database; and our email service provider, which sends out order confirmations.
